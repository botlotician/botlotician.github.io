2026 Promise Tracker: Hollister & California

A nonpartisan list of what candidates on the Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026 ballot in Hollister and San Benito County say they will do, with a link to the source for every promise. It also tracks the status of the current president's 2024 campaign promises. The site records promises as stated and does not endorse any candidate or judge whether a plan is feasible.

Methodology & caveats

2026 candidates

  • What counts as a promise: a specific, forward-looking commitment the candidate made in their own words. Promises are paraphrased or quoted from the linked source. Candidates are listed alphabetically within each race. Party labels are as listed on the ballot. Local offices are nonpartisan.
  • Sources: candidate campaign websites, the California Official Voter Information Guide statements, candidate questionnaires (e.g., GrowSF), and news coverage and Q&As (LA Times, Sacramento Bee, KCRA, ABC7, Daily News, BenitoLink, the Free Lance). Every promise links to its source.
  • Ballot confirmation: statewide and legislative contests come from the CA Secretary of State's Certified List of Candidates (Aug. 27, 2026). Board of Equalization District 2 is confirmed by the SOS BOE district map. Hollister contests are confirmed by the City Clerk elections page. County, school and special-district contests come from Ballotpedia and BenitoLink.
  • County elections site not checked: the San Benito County elections website returned "Access Denied" to automated requests, so local contests could not be checked against the county's own list. Check your sample ballot for the races you will see.
  • Not on the 2026 ballot: California has no U.S. Senate race in 2026. State Senate District 17 (Hollister) is not up this year, because only even-numbered Senate districts are.
  • Voter's Edge (votersedge.org) has shut down and was not used as a source.

    President's 2024 promises

    • Selection: 46 of the most notable, specific promises, drawn mainly from PolitiFact's 75-promise MAGA-Meter, the 2024 Republican platform, Agenda47 videos, and pledges from rallies, interviews and debates.
    • Ratings are PolitiFact-based: Promise Kept → Kept; Compromise → Partially kept; In the Works → In progress; Stalled → Stalled; Broken → Broken. Some notes add newer reporting from the Congressional Research Service, Reuters and CNN. Statuses are as of Oct. 21, 2026.
    • Ratings can be out of date: each one reflects PolitiFact's latest update for that promise, and some were last updated in 2025. Statuses may have changed since then.
    • Dates: some pledges were first made in 2023 and repeated in 2024. For a few promises, the date shown is when PolitiFact listed it (Sept. 30, 2024), not when it was first said. A few dates are approximate.
    • Further context: PBS NewsHour, Feb. 23, 2026; CRS on tariffs, Oct. 2026.
